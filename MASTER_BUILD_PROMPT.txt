CORE PRODUCT
AutoRO-LM is an automotive repair-text intelligence research platform. It converts noisy automotive narratives into structured data and answers automotive questions using retrievable, source-grounded technical documents. It is designed as a public-data research prototype inspired by the automotive LLM/NLP responsibilities in the target job description. It must never claim access to Predii’s proprietary data, internal platform, private models, customers, or systems.

The project has two main AI capabilities:
1. EXTRACTION: Convert unstructured complaint/repair-style text into schema-validated JSON containing vehicle details, symptoms, diagnostic trouble codes (DTCs), components, repair actions, and evidence snippets. Missing or unsupported information must be null/unknown, not hallucinated.
2. GROUNDED DIAGNOSTIC QA: Retrieve relevant public documents, then answer from those passages with verifiable citations. If retrieved evidence does not support an answer, explicitly abstain and explain that evidence is insufficient. A DTC alone must not be presented as proof that a component has failed.

A third differentiator is a SYNTHETIC-DATA RESEARCH PIPELINE: create controlled synthetic technician-style examples grounded in permitted source facts, track provenance, filter low-quality/contradictory examples, fine-tune when hardware/software allow it, and compare synthetic-data mixtures on an untouched real evaluation set.
